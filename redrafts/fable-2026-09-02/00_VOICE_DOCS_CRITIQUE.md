# The voice docs, read cold — 2026-09-02

Fable 5.1, first read of the whole chain (AGENTS → ESSENCE → NICKS_VOICE → SLOP →
INTROS → METHODOLOGY, ~120 KB) plus the five recorded incidents and the memory
notes from earlier sessions. This is a critique and a replacement proposal, not a
rule doc. Nothing here has been applied to the docs. The per-article agents in
this pass were briefed from §4 below.

## 1. The one-paragraph verdict

The docs are a changelog of failures, not a theory of the failure. Every flagged
line in the five incidents is the same defect: a sentence whose purpose was an
effect on the reader (stakes, warmth, suspense, insight, craft) rather than a fact
about the machine. The system answered each incident with a new slop family, so
it now holds twenty-one prohibitions and almost no positive law of the sentence.
A writer briefed on twenty-one prohibitions writes defensively, and defensive prose
is the flat middle METHODOLOGY already warns about. Meanwhile the thing that
actually kills these articles is upstream of voice: most of them are tours, not
stories, and no gate in the chain tests for that.

## 2. What stands

Short, because it matters that these survive any rewrite.

- ESSENCE §3 (design grammar) and §4 (the pedagogy engine). Failure chain, ring,
  planted debts, one-delta figures, phenomenon-before-name. This is the method.
- INTROS constitution rules 1–4 (spend no payoffs, nothing schedules, every
  sentence about the machine, cold open on a concrete thing). These are content
  rules and they are correct.
- SLOP's four tests. Topic-swap, delete, who's-talking, Nick test.
- The sim honesty rules in AGENTS and the Stage-5 reader pass in METHODOLOGY.
- NICKS_VOICE §7, the calibration triptychs. Four target sentences. The only place

## 3. What is wrong, ranked by how much prose it costs

### 3.1 No positive law of the sentence

SLOP is sixteen kilobytes of what not to do. ESSENCE measures another author.
NICKS_VOICE measures Nick talking to a tool, then bans most of it from print. The
blend section is a list of licenses. Nowhere does the chain say the one thing that
generates good prose here.

The law, proposed: **a body sentence states a checkable fact about the object on
screen or in the reader's world, or names the next thing that goes wrong.** The
runnable test is disputability: could a domain expert argue with this sentence?
A schedule, an adjective, a mood, a frame, a recap, a rebrand, a question with no
candidates, a stage direction — none of these can be disputed, so none of them is
content.

That single test covers fifteen of the twenty-one families (1–10, 16, 17, 18, 20,
21). The remaining six are two other tests:

- **Repetition**, at four grains: word (11, another author's tics), device (19,
  our own), skeleton (sibling openings and endings), cadence (12). One rule:
  nothing recurs unless the recurrence pays a planted debt.
- **Verdict**: a name must forbid something (14), an ending must choose (15).
  Process slop (13) is a planning failure, not a prose family.

SLOP.md should be three tests and an appendix of the real before/afters. The
anti-accretion rule in AGENTS ("a new rule should usually replace a worse one")
is being broken by the document that states it.

### 3.2 The second pole is the wrong Nick

NICKS_VOICE measures 7,200 steering prompts. Nick-the-prompter forks, ladders,
names, wants, sends things back to the source. Almost none of that is print, and
§6 says so by banning it. What predicts Nick's verdict on a draft is visible in
the five incidents and is not a voice at all. It is a reader reflex: **he notices
when the sentence is doing something to him.** "At war with noise" does stakes to
him. "Some evening, look at a streetlight" does mood to him. "Here is the
surrender" does theatre. "Just the scoreboard" does insight without performing a
reduction. "Two suspects… neither" does suspense. "One more demonstration belongs
on the table" does craft.

So the second pole is a reader model, not a voice: a reader who feels the author's
hand and withdraws trust the moment he does. Rewrite §6 as that reader. The
transfers that survive (blunt verdicts grounded in the visible, stated wanting,
honest coining, a live fork) survive because they are facts or decisions, not
effects.

### 3.3 ESSENCE prints the recipe it disclaims

§1–2 still carry "Let's ×369," "Notice ×313," "is known as 42+," "Unfortunately
16×/post," the 22-word average, "2–3 deadpan jokes." The preamble says never to
write toward these counts. A model that reads a count anchors on it; that is the
exact mechanism that produced "is known as" at ten times his rate. A disclaimer on
a recipe is still a recipe. Move the numbers to `research/reports/` and keep only
the moves in the agent-facing doc. §3 and §4 lose nothing.

### 3.4 INTROS is the best doc and the next pastiche

The constitution is right. The three-move shape was validated on one article, and
rule 5 fixes the thesis as a fragment at the end. Ten articles opening on
[measured familiar / plain inversion / fragment thesis] is a stamped skeleton;
the sibling audit will catch it within a few articles and family 12 (cadence)
already describes the tell. Keep rules 1–4, 6, 8 as law. Demote the shape to "the
shape that won for p-bits." Let the thesis be a full sentence when a fragment
would be cadence.

### 3.5 METHODOLOGY has no story gate, and story is where these articles die

Stage 1's gate is "the wonder gap is real and the protagonist exists." Stage 2
tests the failure chain per section. Nothing tests the article as a whole. Read
the registry blurbs: "five discoveries, two strangers"; "a tour of checks in
descending strength"; "five things: three bases, one mesh, one topology";
"1858 → 1986." Those are itineraries. The docs cut inventory at section grain and
never notice it at article grain.

The gate, proposed — **the five-sentence test**: write the article as five
declaratives. (1) A thing does X; the reader can see X and cannot explain it.
(2) The obvious mechanism is Y; it fails, and the failure is Z, visible.
(3) Repair W fixes Z and creates V. (4) Repair U fixes V and creates T.
(5) The thing from (1), now predicted by the equation the repairs assembled.
If any sentence begins "then we look at," there is no story yet, and no voice
pass will manufacture one.

Two rules that live only in memory files and belong in Stage 1:

- **Familiarity of the hero is a cost.** "Everyone has seen X" kills a candidate
  (the Mercator/Jacobian postmortem). Ciechanowski's reader needs the daily-
  familiar; this site's reader needs the never-seen.
- **The specimen serves the object.** If most words go to the specimen's own
  physics, it is a host, not a lens.

### 3.6 Three of the four standing deviations are self-inflicted slop sources

- **Waypoints (dev. 3)** are family 8 with a permit. "What you can now do, not
  what was said" collapses into recap in practice. Replacement: a waypoint exists
  only where the next act consumes a tool the last act built, and it states the
  tool as a black box. Otherwise none.
- **Predict-before-reveal (dev. 2)** at one per act is a quiz cadence, and
  "Before you drag, which way will it turn?" is an imperative to the reader,
  which the pronoun regime forbids. Keep only where the common prediction is
  wrong; that is the only case where a prediction teaches.
- **Named solvers (dev. 4)** as standing policy licenses the article to narrate
  its own construction, which is family 18's root. Right for the learned-solver
  lesson, where the scheme is the subject. Elsewhere: one sentence beside the
  figure, never a section.
- **Cash out the math (dev. 1)** is the site's identity. Keep.

### 3.7 The reading order cannot be written from

Seven documents before a word. Nobody holds twenty-one families, fifteen pedagogy
moves, thirteen design rules, nine transfers, nine constitution rules, and five
stages in working memory while composing. The triptychs are the only part of the
chain that shows the target, and there are four.

Replacement: a one-page WRITING.md — the sentence law, the story test, the
repetition test, the verdict test, and twenty to thirty triptychs mined from the
before/afters the docs already record. That is what a writer reads before a draft.
ESSENCE, NICKS_VOICE, SLOP become research, read once.

### 3.8 The reader-ToM review is not in the chain

The best review method the repo has (two simulated readers, line-anchored, felt,
no review vocabulary; validated on lessons 01–03 in July) exists in a memory note
and in `redrafts/`. Stage 4's gate is "the voice audit passes," which is the wrong
gate. Stage 4 should gate on a reader-ToM pass; Stage 5's figure pass stays.

### 3.9 Smaller

- The Nick test as written ("instantiates a rule from our own docs") is not
  runnable; every good sentence instantiates some rule. Disputability is the
  runnable form.
- "Let's" survives when the next clause is a concrete act. The general principle
  is never stated: a discourse word is legal when what follows it is a fact.
- ESSENCE §8.5 still owes a colour-fallback story. Not a prose issue.

## 4. The brief given to the per-article agents

Every article agent in this pass was told to work from these, in this order, and
to treat the existing docs as background:

1. **Sentence law.** Every body sentence is a checkable fact about the object or
   the next thing that goes wrong. Test: could an expert dispute it?
2. **No performance.** A sentence whose job is an effect (stakes, warmth,
   suspense, insight, craft, significance) is cut. The reader supplies the mood.
3. **Story gate.** The five-sentence test must pass before an outline is written.
   Tours, surveys, and "five things" shapes fail it.
4. **Hero cost.** Familiar hero kills; specimen serves the object; the hero
   figure at the top is not yet understandable and returns understood.
5. **Repetition.** No skeleton, device, or cadence shared with a sibling article.
   Nothing recurs unless the recurrence pays a planted debt.
6. **Intro.** INTROS rules 1–4, 6, 8 are law. The three-move shape is optional.
   No "imagine," no metaphor needing an audit, no aphorism that opens.
7. **Review form.** The critique of the current article is a felt reader
   simulation, line-anchored, in the reader's head, with no review vocabulary.
   Story diagnosis before prose diagnosis.
8. **Do not edit the lessons.** Output is one new file per article.
