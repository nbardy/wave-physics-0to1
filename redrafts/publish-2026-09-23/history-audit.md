### Spine verdict

**Holds end to end after the Sept 17 cuts.** The chain is: statics (rest only) →
Leonardo (no rule for "next") → Newton (the guess; corpuscles fail) → Bernoulli
(ledger, no field) → d'Alembert (the mathematics, and the zero) → Euler (the
field; zero inherited) → Navier (the term) → five derivations → Stokes measures
ν → Reynolds (when ν matters) → Prandtl (where the zero came from) → cascade →
smoothness open → hero again with the meter. Removing the barometer tilt and
sound left no dangling reference: L92–93 carries the tilt as one clause, L195–196
carries sound as one sentence, and nothing later leans on either. The pressure
chapter has one instrument (Périer) and one use (Euler sets pressure free at
L328–330), so the section still earns its place.

The two planted debts are both paid: "nothing takes a cut" (L294–296) →
"something takes a cut" (L617–619); "the eight pages" (L597) → "This is what the
eight pages contained" (L634). The back-hill plant is paid at L617 ("Recall the
parcel that had to climb the back hill").

**HANDOFF open item — drag on one object (disc vs wing).** Verdict: it holds, by
one sentence. The drag story runs on the *disc* from Newton (CorpuscleHail, L183)
through d'Alembert (IdealFlow, L271) to Prandtl (Loupe, L605); the *wing* carries
the hero bookends (L34, L757) and the volume-balance figure (L361), which is not

the circle below") — good, and L219 "our gray disc" and L599 "on the disc" then
read naturally. The wing's meter and the loupe's meter are both labelled
"pressure drag" (hero: `pressure drag`; loupe: `Pressure drag Cd`), so the hero's
finale (L759–764) reads as the same quantity on a second body. Nothing to add;
one COULD in §2 (C-11) if Nick wants the second body named at the return.

**HANDOFF open item — "Five Times" / "five discoveries" vs the six-notch hero.**
Verdict: reconciles, but the reader does the arithmetic. "Five" everywhere in
the prose means the five derivations of the equation (Navier, Cauchy, Poisson,
Saint-Venant, Stokes): L27–28 "three other men … in between" (2+3), L436–437

discoverers", L512 "any of the five", L751 "All five discoverers", L770

fluid* (Newton, Euler, Navier, Stokes, Reynolds, Prandtl), and the caption says
so at L55–56 ("The dates mark changes in our understanding, not six
simulations"). Only two of the six (Navier, Stokes) are among the five. The
registry blurb (`src/lessons/registry.ts` L308: "Five discoveries, two strangers
in the name, a 152-year paradox…") uses "discoveries" in the prose's sense and is
consistent with the article; it does not describe the hero. No conflict to fix.
One COULD (C-6) makes the count explicit at L446 so a reader who counts notches
is answered on the spot. Note the HANDOFF's own phrase "six-discovery hero" is
internal shorthand and should not leak into prose.
