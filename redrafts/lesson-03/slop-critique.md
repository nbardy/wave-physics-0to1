## PART 3 — PROPOSAL AUDIT (missing-moves.md)

**1. Kolmogorov (headline fix) — AMENDED.** Approved in structure (verse →
eve-of-war scene → exponent → 1962 sea measurement; Heisenberg aside moved after).
Four amendments, all mandatory:
  (a) *Physics error in the draft:* "a spectrum falling as the −5/3 power of
  eddy size" is wrong — E(k) ∝ k^(−5/3) falls with **wavenumber**; against eddy
  *size* the exponent's sign flips. Bind the exponent to wavenumber or phrase
  neutrally ("energy shared out among eddy sizes by one rule — the −5/3 power
  law, written against the eddies' wavenumber"). Same fix in the 1962 sentence:
  "track −5/3 across decades of **wavenumber**" (the paper's own phrase is
  "several decades in k").
  (b) *Include the Obukhov clause* the proposal offers as optional — one
  half-parenthesis crediting the spectrum form to Kolmogorov's student, same
  school, same year. It is house bookkeeping and it rhymes, unremarked, with the
  eponymy-lottery theme.
  (c) *Cut the closing rhyme* "written in Moscow, in winter … holding in a cold
  channel": the winter/cold pairing is staged poignancy (family 17's sentence-
  order surprise) and "in winter" is shaky for the April paper. Flat version:
  "A rule written at a Moscow desk from a dimensional argument, holding in a
  tidal channel on the far side of the world."
  (d) *REJECT the optional Leonardo callback clause.* The Leonardo ladder
  ("a name … an equation … a number") planted at L94 paid in full at Re, and
  Priya's journey records her feeling exactly that payment. Family 19 allows one
