## Core

A field assigns a value in a fiber at each position. A connection permits
comparisons between nearby fibers. For charged quantum amplitudes, its transport
phases connect to electromagnetic potentials; loop transport has measurable
interference consequences. Maxwell dynamics is an additional physical law, and
its vacuum solutions include light. This is a common mathematical framework,
not a deduction of all physics from conventions or a replacement physical aether.

## Sequence and experiments

1. Interference: add two complex amplitudes; vary relative phase; compare aligned
   reference and selected resultant/intensity. Introduce amplitude before phase frames.
2. Bundle anatomy: allowed values versus selected section, then distinguish complex
   line fibers from circles of unit phase. No claim that all bundles are products.
3. Neighboring values: simultaneous original/regauged rows; compare ordinary and
   transported differences. Derive the link transformation and continuum D.
4. Loops: vary physical flux separately from local bases; product invariant, output
   intensity changes only with flux. Explain AB and excluded-region Stokes condition.
5. Dynamics: derive a vacuum plane-wave system from Maxwell, evolve E/B numerically;
   compare live fields against initial pulse. Explain connection/curvature mapping.

## Accuracy boundaries

Spinless charged amplitude; no derivation of quantum mechanics. Unit phase slices
omit variable magnitude. Links run i→j; compare ψj−Uijψi. D=d−ia and a'=a+dχ.
The four-link loop computes holonomy and two-path interference algebra; it is not
an electron trajectory solver. Maxwell uses c=1, periodic domain, fixed-step RK4;
fields do not establish dynamics merely by being represented geometrically.
