01 · Geometry basis

B-spline

A compactly supported basis for a finite-dimensional function space—closer to a high-continuity finite-element basis than to a mysterious CAD curve.

Curve evaluation

C(u) = ∑ⁿᵢ₌₀ Ni,p(u) Pi 
C(u)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}N_{i,p}(u)P_i

What the symbols do

The basis functions are nonnegative, piecewise polynomial, compactly supported, and form a partition of unity. The curve is therefore a local weighted average of nearby . For degree p, at most p + 1 basis functions are normally active inside one knot span.

Knots partition parameter space

They set polynomial joins, support intervals, continuity, and where additional control becomes available.

Multiplicity controls continuity

An interior knot of multiplicity r in degree p generally gives continuity Cp−r.

Knot insertion is exact refinement

Add a knot and new control points without changing the represented curve. Try it in the visual.

Cox–de Boor recurrence
Ni,0(u)=1 on [ui,ui+1) and 0 elsewhere.
Ni,p(u)= (u−ui)Ni,p−1(u)ui+p−ui + (ui+p+1−u)Ni+1,p−1(u)ui+p+1−ui+1

Zero-over-zero terms are interpreted as zero.

02 · Rational basis

NURBS

A NURBS curve is a rational B-spline: the same local spline basis plus a positive weight at each control point and a normalization denominator.

Rational curve

C(u) = ∑ Ni,p(u) wi Pi ∑ Ni,p(u)wi 
C(u)=\frac{\sum_iN_{i,p}(u)w_iP_i}{\sum_iN_{i,p}(u)w_i}

In homogeneous coordinates, evaluate an ordinary B-spline through P̃i=(wiPi,wi) and project back. That rational projection is what lets finite NURBS exactly represent conics such as circles and ellipses.

A weight is not “importance” in the abstract.

Increasing wi changes the normalized rational blend and pulls the curve toward Pi. Use the vertical handles in the visual.

The tensor-product catch

On a rectangular NURBS surface, inserting a knot in one parameter direction normally inserts a complete control-grid line across the patch. The desired edit may be local; the representation refinement propagates. That is the pressure that motivated T-splines.

03 · Local spline structure

T-spline

A spline surface whose control-grid lines may terminate in T-junctions, allowing local refinement without forcing every new line across the entire patch.

Anchor-local rational basis

S(u,v) = ∑A wAPA NA(u)MA(v) ∑AwANA(u)MA(v) 
S(u,v)=\frac{\sum_A w_AP_A N_A(u)M_A(v)}{\sum_Aw_A N_A(u)M_A(v)}

Each anchor obtains local knot vectors from the surrounding T-mesh rather than from one global rectangular index pair. The combinatorics matter: arbitrary T-junctions do not automatically preserve linear independence, partition of unity, nestedness, or analysis compatibility.

04 · Recursive limit surface

Subdivision surface / SubD

A smooth surface defined as the limit of repeatedly refining a coarse polygonal control mesh.

Catmull–Clark vertex update

P′ = F̄ + 2Ē + (n−3) P n 
P'=\frac{\bar F+2\bar E+(n-3)P}{n}

Face points average each face. Interior edge points average their two endpoints and two neighboring face points. Old vertices move according to the rule above. Each polygon is replaced with quads; repeating the process approaches the limit.

Regular region

At valence four, Catmull–Clark agrees with a bicubic uniform B-spline surface.

Extraordinary vertex

Other valences permit arbitrary mesh topology; smoothness analysis uses the subdivision matrix.

SubD is an exceptional authoring mechanism for organic forms and arbitrary cage topology. Exact radii, analytic cylinders, trimming, robust booleans, and solid semantics belong to other layers.

05 · Boundary topology

B-rep

A boundary representation is not a surface basis. It is an oriented topological structure saying which bounded curves and surfaces form the boundary of a solid.

Topological containment

Solid ⊃ Shell = ⋃ Facej
∂Face = Wireouter ⊔ Wireinner 
\mathrm{Solid}\supset\mathrm{Shell}=\bigcup_j\mathrm{Face}_j

\partial\mathrm{Face}=\mathrm{Wire}_{outer}\sqcup\mathrm{Wire}_{inner}

A face is a bounded region of an underlying surface. An edge is a bounded interval of a curve. A wire is an oriented loop of edges. A shell is an oriented connected set of faces. A valid closed shell can bound a solid.

Geometry and topology are deliberately distinct.

The top face in the visual lies on an infinite plane, but its outer and inner wires select only a finite planar region with a hole.

Why booleans are hard

A B-rep boolean intersects support surfaces, constructs intersection curves, splits faces and edges, classifies fragments, rebuilds shells, and verifies orientation and closure. Tangencies, near-coincidences, slivers, and tolerance-scale features make that pipeline numerically delicate.

The durable mental model

Basis, authoring, and topology are separate axes.

B-spline / NURBS: finite coordinates for parametric functions.

T-spline: a locally refinable spline support structure.

SubD: a smooth limit generated from recursive cage refinement.

B-rep: a topological boundary structure whose entities reference geometry.
