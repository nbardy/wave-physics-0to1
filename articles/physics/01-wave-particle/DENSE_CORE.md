# Dense core — Is Light a Wave or a Particle?

The compressed inspiration. Written first; wins conflicts with later drafts.

## Thesis

The wave/particle question is not answered by picking a side, and not answered by
"both" either. It is answered by noticing that the two words name two *behaviours*
of one arithmetic — amplitudes that add like arrows, arrivals counted by the
square — and that the exchange rate between those behaviours is exact, which is
why no experiment has ever caught the contradiction in the act.

## Hook (Nick's, verbatim, from the commissioning message)

> *Is light made of particles, or of waves?*
> — Quantum mechanics says: both.
> *How can it be both?*
> — It can't. But it is.

Deployed once, at full strength, as the epigraph. It does not recur. (SLOP §19:
a coined device gets one deployment; a refrain is shtick.) It survives SLOP §7
and §17 because the fork is live — the candidates are the two answers the
subject actually had, and the article calls the winner.

## Payoff

$V^2 + D^2 \le 1$, met as a *measured* fact rather than a slogan: the reader
drags a knob from "know nothing about the path" to "know exactly", watches the
fringes drain away continuously, and sees the marker unable to leave a quarter
circle. That is the sentence "it can't, but it is" turned into bookkeeping.

## Ranked insights

1. **Opening a second slit makes some places darker — absolutely, not
   proportionally.** At $x = \lambda L/2d$ the two-slit rate is 2% of the
   one-slit rate at the same point, where the one-slit pattern is at 97% of its
   own peak. This is the fact no collection of independent lumps can produce, and
   it is visible in the hero as one marked column crossing two screens.
2. **Interference relocates light; it never makes or destroys it.** Peak ratio
   4:1, total ratio 2:1, both measured off the tally. The excess in the bars is
   exactly what is missing from the stripes.
3. **The photoelectric effect kills the wave account by a knob that does
   nothing.** Brightness moves the count and not the energy. The classical
   prediction is drawn as a ghost over the bar so the disagreement is one glance.
4. **Ray optics and the far-field sinc² are two corners of one integral.** The
   slit figure runs the exact Fresnel integral across the whole slider, so no
   theory switches under the reader; $a^2/\lambda L$ is the only thing that
   decides which corner you are standing in.
5. **λ = h/p is not about light.** 29 decades of ruler, ending at a baseball
   whose fringes are $10^{-15}$ of a proton wide. Classical mechanics is this
   arithmetic seen from the far end.
6. **"It works" has a size.** The electron magnetic moment, thirteen digits.

## What this article is NOT

- Not an interpretations tour. Copenhagen/many-worlds/pilot-wave are not
  adjudicated here and are not mentioned; the article's verdict is about what the
  bench forces, not about what it means.
- Not a history. The dates present (1905, 1916, 1924, 1927, 1996, 1999) are load
  bearing for specific claims, not a chronology.
- No `<Q>` second-voice layer past the epigraph (AGENTS decision, 2026-07-06).
